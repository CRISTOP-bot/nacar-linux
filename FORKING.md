# Forking and remastering

Forks and independent variants are welcome. This repository deliberately separates build logic from the future distribution identity.

1. Create a fork under an identity and repository name you are entitled to use.
2. Choose a distinct name, logos, artwork, domain, and package branding. Do not imply endorsement or reuse Debian or other third-party marks outside their permitted terms.
3. Keep the original copyright and license notices. Preserve notices for every third-party component and meet its redistribution/source obligations.
4. Review `THIRD_PARTY.md` and the exact license and copyright files in each binary package you redistribute. A package inventory is not a license grant.
5. Record your changes to upstream-derived files, and maintain your own third-party and release inventories.
6. Keep your configuration, brand assets, package policy, and original code distinguishable; do not erase attribution to make a fork appear to be upstream.
7. Publish build instructions, supported suites, security contact, and honest test results for your variant.

The repository uses Nácar GNU/Linux as a provisional working name (`nacar-linux` repository slug; `nacar.iso` image filename); trademark clearance and official artwork are not established. Forks should choose their own identity and keep it separate from the Debian/OpenRC engineering core without erasing upstream attribution.
